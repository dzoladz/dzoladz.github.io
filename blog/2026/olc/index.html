Legendary Services of OPLIN

Created in October 24, 2026

2026   ·   conferences

This year’s OLC (Ohio Library Council) Convention and Expo was held at the Kalahari Resort & Convention Center in Sandusky, Ohio. Kalahari is a massive complex, with Ohio’s largest indoor waterpark (173,000 sq ft) and an easy-to-get-lost-in convention center (215,000 sq ft). If the signage and thematic details didn’t make it obvious that I was at a waterpark, I might not have noticed. No chlorine scent or excessive humidity in any of the spaces.

I’m always exhausted by the continual stream of conversations at the OPLIN booth. But I was able to have a productive conversation with Joe Welps from TBS – Today’s Business Solutions about integrating OPLIN Mask for authentication with TBS products. I was also able to have a conversation with Melissa Lattanzi of NEO-RLS about their upcoming Technology Conference. The conference will be focusing on artificial intelligence (AI) with a morning keynote from Alison Macrina of Library Freedom Project.

Legendary Services of OPLIN – Below is a selection of photos from our booth. We created a deck of Magic: The Gathering style cards to promote the services we offer to Ohio’s public libraries. The cards were beloved by attendees, sparking joy and conversations about OPLIN services. The cards were sustainably printed on post-consumer waste stocks using high-quality soy-based inks by greenprinter.
