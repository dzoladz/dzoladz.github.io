This year's OLC ([Ohio Library Council](https://www.olc.org/)) Convention and Expo  was held at the [Kalahari Resort & 
Convention Center]((https://www.kalahariresorts.com/ohio)) in Sandusky, Ohio. Kalahari is a massive complex, with Ohio's 
largest indoor waterpark (173,000 sq ft) and an easy-to-get-lost-in convention center (215,000 sq ft). If the signage 
and thematic details didn't make it obvious that I was at a waterpark, I might not have noticed. No chlorine scent or 
excessive humidity in any of the spaces.

I'm always exhausted by the continual stream of conversations at the OPLIN booth. But I was able to have a 
productive conversation with Joe Welps from [TBS – Today’s Business Solutions](https://tbsit360.com) about integrating 
[OPLIN Mask](https://oplin.ohio.gov/services/authentication) for authentication with TBS products. I was also able to 
have a conversation with Melissa Lattanzi of [NEO-RLS](https://www.neo-rls.org/) about their upcoming 
[Technology Conference](https://mms.neo-rls.org/Calendar/moreinfo.php?eventid=224158). The conference will be focusing 
on artificial intelligence (AI) with a morning keynote from Alison Macrina of [Library Freedom Project](https://libraryfreedomproject.org/).

**Legendary Services of OPLIN** – Below is a selection of photos from our booth. We created a deck of *Magic: The Gathering* 
style cards to promote the services we offer to Ohio's public libraries. The cards were beloved by attendees, sparking 
joy and conversations about OPLIN services. The cards were sustainably printed on post-consumer waste stocks using high-quality soy-based inks 
by [greenprinter](https://www.greenerprinter.com/).
